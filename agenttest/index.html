Questions

Still unsure? Ask the agent in the corner. It's Fence, selling Fence.

What counts as a conversation?

One visitor chat, up to 20 visitor messages. Unused conversations don't roll over. If you reach your monthly limit, the chat bubble hides until next month unless you upgrade.

Can the agent make up prices or discounts?

No. It only quotes the plans, prices and facts you put in your fact sheet. If a visitor asks something that isn't there, it says so and points them to your contact.

How does the self-learning playbook work?

On Growth and Scale, Fence regularly compares chats that reached checkout with chats that didn't, and rewrites its sales playbook: which questions to ask, how to answer common objections, and when to close. It never adds new facts.

Will visitors know they're talking to an AI?

Yes. The chat is labeled "AI assistant", and the agent says it's an AI if anyone asks.

How do I install it?

Paste one script tag on your pricing page. The widget only runs on domains you approve, so nobody else can embed your agent.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel anytime from billing settings. Yearly billing costs about 17% less than monthly. There's no free trial right now.
